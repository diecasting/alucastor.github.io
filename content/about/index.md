Alucastor is the manufacturing brand of **Dongguan Baiqiang Metal Products Co., Ltd.** — an aluminum casting manufacturer operating a 12,000 m² foundry and machining plant in Dongguan, Guangdong Province, China. Tooling design, melting and degassing, casting, heat treatment, CNC machining, surface finishing and inspection are all performed under one roof, so a single partner is accountable for the finished aluminum component.

## Company Overview

Alucastor runs 28 casting machines and 36 CNC machines and produces around 3,000 tonnes of aluminum components each year. The plant serves customers in automotive, aerospace, electronics, industrial equipment, robotics, new energy and consumer products, and communicates with buyers in English, German, Japanese, French and Spanish.

The business is built around a single accountable workflow: from design for manufacturability (DFM) review through tooling, casting, heat treatment, machining and final inspection. Keeping every process step in-house shortens lead times and keeps dimensional and material quality under direct control.

## Manufacturing Capabilities

Alucastor covers the full aluminum component value chain in-house:

- **Gravity die casting** in reusable H13 permanent molds for dense, pressure-tight parts.
- **Aluminum sand casting** using resin, green and 3D-printed sand molds for large, complex or low-volume parts.
- **Low pressure die casting** for high-integrity components suitable for T6 heat treatment and welding.
- **Precision CNC machining** on 3/4/5-axis machining centres and turning centres.
- **Rapid prototyping** through CNC and casting prototype routes with no tooling investment.
- **Supporting processes** — H13 tooling, aluminum melting and degassing, T6 heat treatment, surface finishing, and inspection with Zeiss CMM, spectrometer, X-ray, dye penetrant and leak testing.

Key equipment includes H13 permanent mold tooling shops, gravity die casting cells, low pressure die casting machines, a resin sand molding line, 3D sand printing, aluminum melting and degassing furnaces, T6 heat treatment furnaces, 3/4/5-axis CNC machining centres, CNC turning centres, Zeiss CMM and spectrometer, X-ray and dye penetrant NDT, and helium/air leak test benches.

## Engineering Support

Every enquiry is reviewed by an engineer, not a sales inbox. Alucastor provides DFM reviews, alloy and process selection, tooling design and prototyping support so that the specification is settled before tooling is committed.

Customers can submit a 3D model in STEP or IGES format with a 2D drawing, the target alloy or load case, annual volume, critical tolerances and surface finish, and receive a manufacturability assessment and a quotation — typically within one business day. If the alloy is still open, the engineering team will recommend one from the supported grades: ADC12, A380, A356, AlSi7Mg, AlSi10Mg, 6061 and 7075.

## Quality Commitment

Quality management follows **ISO 9001** and **IATF 16949**, with **ISO 14001** environmental certification and RoHS/REACH material documentation available on request. Dimensional control combines in-process checks with final verification on Zeiss coordinate measuring machines and spectrometer analysis, supported by X-ray, dye penetrant NDT and helium/air leak testing where the application requires it.

Sample lead time is around 15 days, and first-article and production inspection reports are provided against the customer drawing and referenced standards.

## Global OEM Partnership

Alucastor supplies aluminum components to OEMs and tier suppliers across automotive, aerospace, electronics, industrial equipment, robotics, new energy and consumer products. The plant is roughly 90 minutes by car from Shenzhen Bao'an and Guangzhou Baiyun international airports and about one hour from Hong Kong, China via the Shenzhen Bay crossing.
