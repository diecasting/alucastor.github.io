## 7075 Aluminum Alloy

7075 is the high-strength member of the aluminum family — an Al-Zn-Mg-Cu aerospace alloy whose strength-to-weight ratio rivals many steels at roughly one third of the mass. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China** with a full [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) division, Alucastor machines 7075 for [prototype](/services/prototype/) and production parts that must carry serious load.

Where 6061 is the generalist, 7075 is the specialist: it is chosen when yield strength and fatigue resistance decide whether a component survives. Its excellent machinability to a fine surface finish makes it a favourite for precision structural and tooling work.

## Key Properties

- **Chemical composition:** Zn 5.1 – 6.1 %, Mg 2.1 – 2.9 %, Cu 1.2 – 2.0 %
- **Tensile strength (T6):** 572 MPa
- **Yield strength (T6):** 503 MPa
- **Elongation:** 5 – 11 %
- **Brinell hardness:** approximately 150 HB
- **Excellent fatigue strength** for cyclic loading
- **Good machinability** with excellent surface finish
- **Limited weldability** — mechanical fastening preferred

## Composition and Standards

- Grades: 7075-T6, 7075-T651, 7075-T7351
- Designations: EN AW-7075, UNS A97075, AMS 4045

## Applications

- Aerospace structural fittings and airframe parts
- High-load robotic and automation components
- Motorsport and performance vehicle parts
- Precision jigs, fixtures and mold plates
- Defence and high-stress industrial hardware

## Processing Notes

7075 stock is verified on receipt and machined on 3, 4 and 5-axis centres to ±0.005 mm where required; stress-relieved tempers (T651 / T7351) control movement on tight-tolerance parts. Anodizing (including hard coat) and other finishing are performed in-house. Because weldability is limited, design for mechanical fastening is recommended.
