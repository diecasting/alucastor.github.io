## ADC12 Aluminum Alloy

ADC12 is the most widely specified aluminum die casting alloy in the world — an Al-Si-Cu grade chosen wherever thin walls, high volume and cost efficiency matter more than post-cast heat treatment. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor casts ADC12 by the tonne across its [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/), [low pressure die casting](/services/low-pressure-die-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) routes.

Its high silicon content delivers outstanding fluidity, so ADC12 fills complex, thin-wall cavities cleanly and holds tight dimensional stability shot after shot. That combination of castability and consistency is why it dominates high-volume consumer, automotive and electronics housings.

## Key Properties

- **Chemical composition:** Si 9.6 – 12.0 %, Cu 1.5 – 3.5 %, Fe ≤ 1.3 %
- **Tensile strength:** 228 – 310 MPa
- **Yield strength:** 150 – 170 MPa
- **Brinell hardness:** 80 – 90 HB
- **Melting range:** 515 – 582 °C
- **Excellent fluidity** — supports wall thickness down to 1.0 mm
- **Good machinability and dimensional stability**
- **Not recommended for T6 heat treatment** (copper content)

## Composition and Standards

- Grades: ADC12, A383 equivalent, YL113
- Designations: JIS H5302 ADC12, ASTM A383, GB YL113

## Applications

- Automotive engine and transmission covers
- Electronic housings and connector shells
- Power tool and appliance bodies
- Telecom heat sinks and RF enclosures

## Processing Notes

ADC12 is verified by optical emission spectrometer on every heat. Castings are fettled, shot blasted and may be CNC machined in-house where functional faces require tight tolerance. The alloy is finished by powder coating, painting or plating; anodizing is less common than for 6000-series wrought grades.
