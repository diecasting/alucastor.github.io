## Key Properties of Aluminum

Aluminum is selected across industries because of a rare combination of properties:

- **Density:** 2.66 – 2.81 g/cm³ (roughly one third of steel) — the foundation of lightweight design.
- **Tensile strength:** 150 – 570 MPa depending on alloy and temper.
- **Thermal conductivity:** 96 – 167 W/m·K — excellent for heat sinks and thermal management.
- **Corrosion resistance:** a passive oxide layer gives natural protection without coating.
- **Non-magnetic and non-sparking** — important for electronics and hazardous environments.
- **100 % recyclable** at approximately 5 % of the primary energy cost.
