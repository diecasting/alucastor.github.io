## 6061 Aluminum Alloy

6061 is the most versatile wrought aluminum alloy for machined components — an Al-Mg-Si grade that balances strength, weldability, corrosion resistance and anodizing quality better than any other common alloy. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China** with a full [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) division, Alucastor machines 6061 extensively for [prototype](/services/prototype/) and production parts.

Its excellent machinability, clean chip breaking and uniform anodized finish make it the natural choice whenever a part is cut from billet rather than cast, and its weldability lets fabrications be built up from machined elements.

## Key Properties

- **Chemical composition:** Mg 0.8 – 1.2 %, Si 0.4 – 0.8 %, Cu 0.15 – 0.40 %
- **Tensile strength (T6):** 310 MPa
- **Yield strength (T6):** 276 MPa
- **Elongation:** 12 – 17 %
- **Brinell hardness:** approximately 95 HB
- **Excellent machinability** with clean chip breaking
- **Readily weldable** by TIG and MIG
- **Uniform, high-quality anodized finish**

## Composition and Standards

- Grades: 6061-T6, 6061-T651, 6061-T4
- Designations: EN AW-6061, UNS A96061, GB 6061

## Applications

- CNC machined prototypes and production parts
- Robotic frames, plates and brackets
- Electronic and instrument enclosures
- Optical and semiconductor equipment parts

## Processing Notes

6061 stock is verified on receipt and machined on 3, 4 and 5-axis centres to ±0.005 mm where required. Stress-relieved tempers (T651) minimise movement on tight-tolerance parts. Finishing — anodizing, powder coating, chromating, polishing — is performed in-house, with CMM and vision inspection confirming critical features.
