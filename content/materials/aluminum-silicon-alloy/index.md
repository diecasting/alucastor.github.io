## Aluminum Silicon Alloy

Hypoeutectic aluminum-silicon alloys — A356, AlSi7Mg, AlSi10Mg and AlSi12 — are the backbone of high-integrity aluminum casting. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor casts these grades across its [low pressure die casting](/services/low-pressure-die-casting/), [sand casting](/services/sand-casting/), [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/) and [prototyping](/services/prototype/) routes.

What makes the Al-Si family special is the balance it strikes: silicon gives outstanding fluidity and low shrinkage porosity, magnesium enables a powerful T6 precipitation hardening response, and the low copper content keeps the alloy weldable. The result is castings that are dense, strong, and capable of being joined into larger structures.

## Key Properties

- **Chemical composition:** Si 6.5 – 12.0 %, Mg 0.25 – 0.45 %, low Cu
- **As-cast tensile strength:** 160 – 200 MPa
- **T6 tensile strength:** 280 – 320 MPa
- **T6 elongation:** 8 – 12 %
- **Outstanding fluidity and low shrinkage porosity**
- **Fully weldable** — suitable for fabricated assemblies
- **Superior corrosion resistance** versus copper-bearing alloys
- **Preferred alloy family for low pressure die casting**

## Composition and Standards

- Grades: AlSi7Mg, AlSi10Mg, AlSi12, A356.2
- Designations: EN AC-42000, EN AC-43000, EN AC-44100, ASTM A356

## Processing Notes

Al-Si-Mg castings are verified by optical emission spectrometer and typically T6 heat treated — solution treatment, quench and artificial ageing — to reach specified mechanical properties. X-ray or dye penetrant inspection confirms internal soundness for safety-relevant parts, and leak testing verifies pressure tightness.
