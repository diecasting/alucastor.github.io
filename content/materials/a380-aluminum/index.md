## A380 Aluminum Alloy

A380 is the general-purpose aluminum casting alloy that engineers reach for when a component must be strong, pressure-tight and stable at temperature. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor casts A380 across its [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/), [sand casting](/services/sand-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) routes.

Its balanced Al-Si-Cu composition delivers good fluidity for thin sections, high resistance to hot cracking, and mechanical properties that hold up better than many alloys under elevated service temperature — which is exactly what gearbox, hydraulic and powertrain housings demand.

## Key Properties

- **Chemical composition:** Si 7.5 – 9.5 %, Cu 3.0 – 4.0 %, Fe ≤ 1.3 %
- **Tensile strength:** 315 – 330 MPa
- **Yield strength:** 160 – 165 MPa
- **Elongation:** 3 – 4 %
- **Brinell hardness:** approximately 80 HB
- **Melting range:** 540 – 595 °C
- **Excellent pressure tightness** for fluid-carrying parts
- **Retains strength at elevated service temperature**

## Composition and Standards

- Grades: A380, A380.0, ADC10 equivalent
- Designations: ASTM B85 A380, UNS A03800, JIS ADC10

## Applications

- Gearbox and transmission housings
- Hydraulic and pneumatic valve bodies
- Engine brackets and mounting plates
- Pump casings and manifold blocks
- Industrial equipment frames

## Processing Notes

A380 is verified by optical emission spectrometer on every heat. Castings can be T5 heat treated for dimensional stability and finished by powder coating, painting or plating. Leak testing confirms pressure tightness for fluid-carrying duty.
