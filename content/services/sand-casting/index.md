## Aluminum Sand Casting Services in China

Sand casting is the most flexible aluminum casting process available, and at Alucastor it is the route we recommend whenever a component is large, geometrically complex, or required in quantities that cannot justify hard tooling. A sand mold is destroyed after each pour, which sounds inefficient until you consider what it buys: unlimited internal complexity through sand cores, castings weighing half a tonne, and a first article in the time it would take merely to quote a steel die.

As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor runs resin (furan) sand, green sand and binder-jet 3D printed sand molding lines in the same Dongguan facility as our [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) departments — so a sand casting leaves the plant heat treated, machined, inspected and ready to install.

## Manufacturing Process

1. **Pattern design review and methoding simulation.** Gating, risering and solidification are simulated from your 3D model so that shrinkage is fed correctly before any pattern is built.
2. **Pattern and core box production.** Patterns are produced in wood, resin or aluminum depending on expected volume — or skipped entirely when 3D printed sand molds are used.
3. **Mold preparation.** Furan resin sand is used for dimensional accuracy and surface finish; green sand for higher-volume, lower-cost work; binder-jet printed sand for zero-tooling prototypes.
4. **Core making and mold assembly.** Sand cores form internal passages, water jackets and undercuts that no permanent mold could release. Cores are set and the mold is closed and clamped.
5. **Melting and treatment.** Aluminum is melted, rotary degassed, grain refined and verified by spectrometer before pouring.
6. **Gravity pouring.** The mold is filled under gravity at a controlled melt temperature with filtration in the running system.
7. **Cooling, shakeout and sand reclamation.** Castings are cooled in mold, shaken out, and the sand is mechanically and thermally reclaimed.
8. **Fettling.** Gates and risers are cut off, the casting is ground, then shot blasted to a uniform finish.
9. **Heat treatment.** T6 solution treatment, quench and artificial ageing where mechanical properties are specified.
10. **Machining and inspection.** CNC machining of functional faces, followed by CMM dimensional inspection, X-ray or dye penetrant NDT, leak testing and packing.

## Advantages

**No hard tooling investment.** A resin sand pattern or a 3D printed mold costs a small fraction of a steel die, so the process is economical from a single piece.

**Very large parts.** Sand casting handles components up to 500 kg that no die casting machine could produce.

**Unlimited internal complexity.** Sand cores create internal passages, cooling channels and undercuts that cannot be released from a permanent mold.

**Fast design iteration.** A pattern can be modified in days; a steel die cannot. This makes sand casting the natural bridge between [prototype](/services/prototype/) and series production.

**Full heat treatment and weldability.** Al-Si-Mg sand castings accept T6 treatment and can be welded into larger fabrications.

**Any alloy.** Because the mold is consumable, alloy changes carry no tooling penalty.
