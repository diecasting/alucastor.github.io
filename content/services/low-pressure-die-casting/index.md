## Low Pressure Die Casting Services from an Aluminum Casting Manufacturer in China

Alucastor is an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China** operating automated low pressure die casting cells in Dongguan, Guangdong. Low pressure die casting fills the die from below, through a riser tube, under a controlled gas pressure — so the molten aluminum rises calmly into the cavity and then solidifies under maintained pressure that feeds every last bit of shrinkage back into the casting.

That controlled, non-turbulent fill is why low pressure die casting produces the densest, lowest-porosity aluminum parts available from a die process. Engineers specify it for components that must carry structural load, seal against pressure, or meet the mechanical properties that only a T6 heat treatment can unlock. Alucastor pairs the process with in-house [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/), heat treatment and inspection so the part ships finished and certified.

## Manufacturing Process

Every low pressure die casting programme at Alucastor follows the same controlled ten-step route:

1. **Feasibility study and simulation.** Your 3D model is assessed for wall thickness, draft and feeding; filling and solidification are simulated before any steel is cut.
2. **Low pressure die design.** The die is engineered around a central riser tube with integrated thermal management, cooling channels and venting.
3. **H13 die manufacture and trial.** The die is CNC machined in H13 hot-work tool steel, fitted, and proved out on a trial run.
4. **Melt preparation.** Charge is melted in a sealed holding furnace, rotary degassed and verified by spectrometer.
5. **Controlled low pressure filling.** Metal is raised through the riser tube at 0.02 to 0.08 MPa — a calm, gas-free fill that keeps oxide and porosity out of the casting.
6. **Pressure-held directional solidification.** Pressure is maintained while the casting freezes progressively from the extremities toward the riser, which feeds shrinkage.
7. **Die opening and automated trimming.** The part is extracted and gates/risers removed, often with automated handling for repeatability.
8. **T6 heat treatment.** Solution treatment, quench and artificial ageing deliver the specified mechanical properties, followed by straightening.
9. **CNC machining.** Sealing faces, bores, threads and mounting features are machined on our own 3, 4 and 5-axis centres.
10. **Leak testing and inspection.** Helium or air leak testing, CMM dimensional verification and export packing.

## Advantages

**Lowest porosity of any die process.** Filling from below under pressure and solidifying under maintained pressure gives castings at ASTM E505 porosity level 1 to 2 — suitable for structural and pressure-carrying duty.

**Full heat treatability.** With no entrapped gas, low pressure castings accept T6 processing and reach 280 to 320 MPa tensile strength with 8 to 12 % elongation.

**Superior consistency.** Automated cells and controlled filling deliver tight, repeatable properties across long runs — exactly what series production and PPAP discipline require.

**Excellent mechanical properties.** A T6-treated Al-Si-Mg low pressure casting matches or exceeds the strength of many gravity and high pressure routes while staying weldable.

**Good wall thickness control.** Sections down to 2.5 mm are fed correctly, enabling lighter components without defect.

**Clean surface for finishing.** The as-cast surface takes anodizing, powder coating and painting cleanly.
