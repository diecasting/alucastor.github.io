## Rapid Prototyping Services from an Aluminum Casting Manufacturer in China

Alucastor is an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China** that runs a dedicated [rapid prototyping](/services/prototype/) service in Dongguan, Guangdong. Prototyping is the bridge between a CAD model and confident series production — and because we operate both CNC machining and every casting process, we can prototype a part by whichever route will eventually make it.

A CNC prototype is machined straight from 6061 or 7075 billet and is the fastest way to validate geometry and fit. A casting prototype is poured from a 3D printed sand mold or rapid soft tooling, giving you a real aluminum casting — with its wall thickness, draft and feeding behaviour — long before a steel die is justified. Both routes deliver production-equivalent material and a full dimensional report.

## Manufacturing Process

Every prototyping programme at Alucastor follows the same controlled ten-step route:

1. **Design review and DFM feedback.** Your model is reviewed for manufacturability and the best prototype route is selected.
2. **CNC prototype machining.** For billet parts, the component is machined directly from 6061 or 7075 stock.
3. **Casting prototype tooling.** For cast parts, a 3D printed sand mold or rapid soft tool is prepared — no hard tooling.
4. **Melting and pouring.** The prototype is cast in the production-equivalent alloy under controlled conditions.
5. **Finish machining.** Functional and mating surfaces are machined to the intended drawing tolerance.
6. **Surface finishing.** The prototype is finished to production appearance standard for fit and customer review.
7. **Dimensional report.** A full measurement report and material certificate are issued with the batch.
8. **Functional testing.** Fit, form and function are verified and fed back to your design team.
9. **Design freeze.** The validated geometry is locked and series tooling is kicked off.
10. **Seamless transfer.** The proven process migrates directly to gravity, low pressure or sand series production.
