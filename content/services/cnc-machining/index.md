## Manufacturing Process

Every CNC machining programme at Alucastor follows the same controlled ten-step route:

1. **Drawing and GD&T review.** Your model and tolerances are reviewed for stack-up, datum strategy and inspection plan.
2. **CAM programming.** Toolpaths, fixtures and soft jaws are designed for repeatability and minimal setup.
3. **Material verification.** Casting blank or billet is confirmed by spectrometer before cutting.
4. **Roughing.** Stock is removed on 3, 4 and 5-axis centres, leaving controlled allowance for finishing.
5. **Stress relief.** Where tight tolerances demand it, parts are stress relieved to prevent movement.
6. **Finish milling and turning.** Critical features are machined to drawing tolerance — bores, faces, threads and contours.
7. **Threading and deburring.** Thread milling, tapping, reaming and clean deburring of all edges.
8. **Surface finishing.** Anodizing, powder coating, bead blasting or passivation to the required appearance and protection.
9. **First article inspection.** FAI with full dimensional report and CMM verification.
10. **SPC and packing.** Statistical process control across the run, then cleaning and protective packing.
