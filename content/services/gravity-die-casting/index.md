## Gravity Die Casting Services from an Aluminum Casting Manufacturer in China

Alucastor is an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China** operating dedicated gravity die casting cells in Dongguan, Guangdong. Gravity die casting — known internationally as permanent mold casting — pours molten aluminum into a reusable H13 steel die under gravity alone. There is no injection ram and no turbulent high-velocity fill, so air is not entrained in the melt and the finished casting is dense throughout its section.

That single metallurgical difference is why engineers specify gravity die casting when a component must hold pressure, carry structural load, be welded into an assembly, or reach the mechanical properties that only a T6 heat treatment can deliver. Alucastor combines the process with in-house tooling, heat treatment, [precision CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) and inspection, so the part you receive is finished, measured and ready for assembly.

## Manufacturing Process

Every gravity die casting programme at Alucastor follows the same controlled ten-step route:

1. **DFM review and casting simulation.** Your 3D model is analysed for wall thickness uniformity, draft, fillet radii and feeding paths. Filling and solidification are simulated before any steel is cut.
2. **Permanent mold design and tooling manufacture.** The die is designed in H13 hot-work tool steel with integrated risers, venting and cooling channels, then CNC machined and heat treated in our own tool room.
3. **Melting, degassing and grain refinement.** Charge material is melted, rotary degassed to remove hydrogen, and treated with grain refiner and modifier to control the silicon morphology.
4. **Mold preheating and coating.** The die is preheated to 250–350 °C and sprayed with a refractory coating that controls heat extraction and drives directional solidification.
5. **Gravity pouring.** Metal is poured statically or by tilt pour at a controlled temperature between 690 °C and 730 °C. The calm fill is what keeps oxide films and entrapped gas out of the casting.
6. **Directional solidification.** Cooling is managed so that the casting freezes progressively from the far extremities toward the risers, which feed the shrinkage.
7. **Shakeout and cleaning.** Parts are extracted, gates and risers removed, then shot blasted to a uniform surface.
8. **Heat treatment.** T5 or T6 solution treatment, quenching and artificial ageing are applied where mechanical properties are specified, followed by straightening.
9. **CNC machining.** Sealing faces, bores, threads and mounting features are machined on our own 3, 4 and 5-axis centres.
10. **Inspection and packing.** Leak testing to 6 bar, CMM dimensional verification, spectrometer material confirmation and export packing.

## Advantages

**Low porosity.** The calm gravity fill avoids the gas entrapment inherent to high pressure die casting, so castings are pressure-tight and can be safely welded.

**Full heat treatability.** Because there is no trapped gas to blister during solution treatment, gravity die castings accept T6 processing and reach substantially higher yield strength and elongation.

**Better mechanical properties.** A T6-treated A356 gravity casting typically reaches 280–320 MPa tensile strength with 8–12 % elongation — figures unavailable from conventional high pressure die casting.

**Economical tooling.** Permanent mold tooling costs a fraction of a high pressure die casting die, making the process viable from a few hundred parts per year upward.

**Thicker sections without defects.** Wall thicknesses from 3.0 mm up to heavy sections can be fed correctly through properly designed risers.

**Excellent surface for finishing.** The as-cast surface takes anodizing, powder coating and painting cleanly.
