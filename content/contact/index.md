## What to Include in Your RFQ

The more of this you can supply up front, the faster and more accurate the quotation:

- **3D model** — STEP or IGES preferred; Parasolid and native CAD are also accepted.
- **2D drawing** — with datums, critical dimensions and tolerance callouts.
- **Aluminum grade** — for example ADC12, A380, A356, AlSi10Mg, 6061 or 7075. If undecided, describe the load case and environment instead.
- **Heat treatment** — as-cast, T5 or T6.
- **Annual quantity and batch size** — this determines whether a permanent mold or a sand pattern is the lower total cost.
- **Surface finish** — as-cast, shot blasted, anodised, powder coated or chromate.
- **Testing requirements** — CMM report, material certificate, X-ray, dye penetrant, leak test, tensile test.
- **Target market** — so packing, documentation and compliance declarations are prepared correctly.

## About Alucastor

Alucastor is an aluminum casting manufacturer operating a 12,000 m² foundry and machining plant in Dongguan, Guangdong. Twenty-eight casting machines and thirty-six CNC machines produce around 3,000 tonnes of aluminum components a year for automotive, aerospace, electronics, industrial equipment, robotics, new energy and consumer product customers.

Tooling design, melting and degassing, gravity die casting, sand casting, low pressure die casting, T5/T6 heat treatment, CNC machining, surface finishing and inspection are all performed in-house. Quality management follows ISO 9001 and IATF 16949, with ISO 14001 environmental certification and RoHS/REACH material documentation available on request.
