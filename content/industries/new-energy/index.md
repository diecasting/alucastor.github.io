## Aluminum Components for the New Energy Industry

The energy transition runs on aluminum — for light weight, thermal performance and sealed integrity. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor supplies aluminum casting and machining for EV, battery, solar and wind applications, focused on thermal performance, pressure tightness and lightweight structural integrity.

Our [low pressure die casting](/services/low-pressure-die-casting/) route produces the dense, sealed enclosures that battery and motor systems require, while [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) deliver cooling plates and structural brackets from the same plant.
