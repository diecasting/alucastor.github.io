## Aluminum Components for the Aerospace Industry

Aerospace is the most demanding proving ground for aluminum — every gram and every property matters, and every part must be traced and inspected. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor supplies high-integrity aluminum castings and precision machined fittings for airframe, UAV and ground support equipment, backed by non-destructive testing and full material traceability.

Our low pressure die casting route delivers the dense, low-porosity Al-Si-Mg structural castings the sector needs, while our [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) division holds the tight tolerances that airframe fittings demand — all under documented process control.
