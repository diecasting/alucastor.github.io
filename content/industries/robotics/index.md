## Aluminum Components for the Robotics Industry

Robotics rewards low inertia and high stiffness in equal measure — and aluminum delivers both. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor produces precision aluminum structural parts for industrial robots, cobots and automation cells, where low inertia, tight tolerances and repeatable stiffness decide how fast and accurately a machine can move.

Our [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) division holds the tolerances that robot joints and mounting plates demand, while our casting routes let larger links and housings be made near-net-shape and finished in the same plant.
