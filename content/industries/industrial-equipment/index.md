## Aluminum Components for Industrial Equipment

Machinery builders need castings that are large, sound and delivered finished — not rough blanks that consume their own machining capacity. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor supplies large and medium aluminum castings for industrial equipment: pump bodies, gearbox casings, compressor housings and structural frames, delivered as finished, machined assemblies.

Our [sand casting](/services/sand-casting/) route handles the biggest envelopes, while [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) deliver medium parts to tight tolerance — all from one accountable plant.
