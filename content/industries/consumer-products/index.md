## Aluminum Components for Consumer Products

Premium consumer goods reward materials that feel substantial yet stay light — and aluminum, finished well, delivers exactly that. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor produces appearance-critical aluminum components for premium consumer goods, kitchen equipment, power tools and outdoor products, finished by anodizing, polishing or powder coating.

Our [gravity die casting](/services/gravity-die-casting/) and [CNC machining](/services/cnc-machining/) routes, combined with in-house finishing, turn raw aluminum into shelf-ready parts with a premium look and a durable surface.
