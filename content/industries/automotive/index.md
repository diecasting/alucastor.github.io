## Aluminum Components for the Automotive Industry

The automotive industry is where aluminum's weight, strength and recyclability pay the biggest dividend — and where process discipline is non-negotiable. As an **aluminum casting manufacturer in China**, Alucastor supplies lightweight aluminum castings and machined components for powertrain, chassis and thermal systems, produced under IATF 16949 discipline with PPAP documentation.

From a single bracket to a high-volume housing, our automotive programmes run on APQP, PPAP and SPC, with material traceability from ingot to finished part. That is what lets us support both internal-combustion and electric-vehicle supply chains from the same plant.
